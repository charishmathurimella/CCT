# Technical support

For a VPN connection problem, confirm that your internet connection works, restart the VPN client, and retry sign-in. If the issue persists, record the error message and contact IT support.

If your laptop does not start, check the power cable and charging indicator before contacting device support. Do not open or repair company-managed hardware yourself.

For meeting audio issues, check the selected microphone and speaker devices in the meeting application's settings.
